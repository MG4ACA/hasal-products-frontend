# Project Summary - Quick Reference

**Client:** Hasal_Products  
**Project:** POS & Inventory Management System  
**Budget:** 300,000 LKR  
**Timeline:** 10-12 weeks  
**Tech:** Vue.js 3 + Express.js + MySQL

---

## What We're Building

A complete web-based POS system for a spices delivery company that:

- Buys raw materials from suppliers (with batch tracking)
- Creates spice products using recipes (with versioning)
- Packages in multiple sizes (100g, 500g, 1kg, etc.)
- Sells to outlets via delivery vehicles
- Manages credit sales and payments
- Tracks inventory and generates reports

## Main Modules (11 Total)

1. **Suppliers** - Manage suppliers, balances, payments (cash/credit/check)
2. **Raw Materials** - Track inventory with system-generated batch numbers
3. **Products & SKUs** - Multiple sizes per product, barcode tracking
4. **Recipes** - Variable recipes with versioning
5. **Production** - Convert raw materials → finished goods
6. **Routes & Outlets** - Customer management by area
7. **Employees** - Track sales refs, drivers, warehouse staff (no login)
8. **Vehicles** - Dynamic route assignment with history
9. **Sales** - Create invoices with returns handling, 20% default discount
10. **Payments** - Collect cash/credit/check from outlets, track clearance
11. **Reports** - Sales, inventory, financial, returns, check status reports
